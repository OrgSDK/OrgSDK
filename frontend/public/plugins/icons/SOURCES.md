- GitHub: unmodified `GitHub_Invertocat_Black.svg` from the official https://brand.github.com/GitHub_Logos.zip. Integration identification and secondary placement: https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo.
- Gmail: official Google artwork from https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/i/productlogos/gmail_2020q4/v8/192px.svg.
- Google Sheets: official Google artwork from https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/i/productlogos/sheets_2020q4/v6/192px.svg.

Provider guidance: https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/facebook/logo/ and https://about.x.com/en/who-we-are/brand-toolkit.
